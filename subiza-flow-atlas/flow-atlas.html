Voice Selection & Cloning

Choose how the agent sounds — from a library, or by cloning a real voice with consent that is genuine, documented and revocable. The highest-legal-risk flow in the product.

Actor

Entry

Exit

Criticality
Critical (legal)
CHOOSE A LIBRARY VOICE30 seconds, in activation, preview says their business name
OR — CLONE A REAL VOICEdeferred to Part Four: needs a second person and a legal artefact
1 Whose voice?no contactable owner = REFUSED, no exception
2 Their consentin their language, revocation stated before they agree
3 Record 30–60 secondsdespite technical minimums of 5–10s
4 ★ VERIFY — read this generated sentence ★proves presence and participation, not identity. We are honest about that

Constraints that shape this flow

  • Industry norm is a checkbox with no verification. We reject that norm
  • A voiceprint is very likely biometric data: unlawful processing carries 7–10 years and RWF 20–25 million
  • Only the voice owner can revoke — even after leaving the business, even if the account owner objects
  • Subiza never impersonates a named human. The cloned voice still says it is an assistant

Success criteria

  • No voice cloned without a verified consent artefact naming the voice owner
  • Revocation disables within 5 minutes and cannot be blocked by the account owner
  • Third-party cloning without a contactable owner is refused, 100%
